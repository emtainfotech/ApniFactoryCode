Cookie Policy
1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit our website. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences (such as login, language, font size, and other display preferences) over a period of time, so you don’t have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back to the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., maintaining your logged-in session, security tokens).
  • Performance & Analytics: We use these to understand how visitors interact with our website, track search history to improve results, and see which products are trending.
  • Marketing: These may be set through our site by our advertising partners to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites.
3. Data Retention

The data collected via cookies is stored securely. Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies (like your search history preferences) remain on your device until you delete them or they expire after a set period (usually 30-90 days).

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Manage Cookies" in the banner or footer. You can also block cookies via your browser settings, though this may impact your experience on our site (e.g., search history may not be saved).
